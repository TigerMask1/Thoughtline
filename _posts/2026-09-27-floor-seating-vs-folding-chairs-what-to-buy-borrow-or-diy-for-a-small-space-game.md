You need to keep everyone close enough to read cards and reach the board, but you also need room for elbows, drinks, and snacks. \n\nThe debate usually settles between two low-footprint setups: gathering around a low coffee table with floor seating, or pulling standard-height folding chairs up to an expandable dining table. Both have distinct trade-offs in comfort, storage, and cost.\n\n## The Core Trade-Off: Posture vs. Storage Footprint\n\nFolding chairs offer familiar, upright support. If you are playing a heavy, three-hour strategy game like *Terraforming Mars* or *Scythe*, an upright seat keeps players engaged and eliminates lower-back fatigue. However, folding chairs demand dedicated storage. Even slim models measure 2 to 3 inches thick when folded; a stack of four consumes a noticeable slice of a hall closet or leans awkwardly against a bedroom wall.\n\nFloor seating transforms your living room into an informal, lounge-style space. It works best around a standard coffee table (typically 16 to 18 inches high). The downside is endurance. Without back support, most adults start shifting, slouching, or leaning against sofas after about 45 minutes. Floor seating excels for casual party games, social deduction titles, or trivia, but struggles during long engine-builders.\n\n## What to Buy: Durable, Stackable Winners\n\nIf you want a permanent host kit that does not swallow your closet, choose based on your primary table setup:\n\n- **For the floor: Japanese floor chairs or firm zafus.** Skip flimsy throw pillows from the couch—they compress to flat fabric within 20 minutes. Instead, pick up firm buckwheat or dense foam [zafu cushions](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zafu), which raise the hips 4 to 6 inches above the knees to take pressure off the spine. If back support is essential, look for folding legless floor chairs (often called *zaisu*), which feature ratcheting metal frames and lay completely flat under a bed when not in use.\n- **For dining tables: Hanging folding chairs.** Avoid bulky padded banquet chairs. Opt for lightweight plastic-and-steel options like the folding models available at [IKEA](https://www.ikea.com), which include molded cutouts on the backrest. These can hang flat from heavy-duty utility hooks installed behind an interior door or along a closet wall, lifting the storage footprint entirely off your floor.\n\n## What to Borrow: Keep It Uniform\n\nBorrowing extra seating is often smarter than buying items that sit in storage 28 days a month. But mismatched heights can ruin a game's sightlines.\n\n- **Borrow camp chairs carefully:** Modern compact backpacking chairs (the style that packs into a 14-inch zippered pouch) are fantastic for small apartments.
